1. Who we are

MushroomSnap is provided by Vasundhara Infotech LLP ("MushroomSnap", "we", "us", or "our"), located at Opp. Nayara Petrol Pump, Singanpore Road, Katargam, Surat 395004, Gujarat, India. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the MushroomSnap mobile application and related pages.

Safety note: Mushroom identification can be wrong. Never eat, touch, prepare, or use a wild mushroom based only on the app's output. Consult a qualified local expert.

2. Information we process

Photos and identification requests

When you choose a photo or use the camera, the selected image is processed to provide an identification result. The image may contain metadata or details from its surroundings. Identification requests may be sent to Google Lens or another configured identification provider. We do not require you to create an account.

Saved content and preferences

Your scan history, saved mushrooms, collections, app language, onboarding status, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device. Scan history may include a local copy of the selected image. You can remove saved content in the app or remove local app data by deleting the app.

Diagnostics, analytics, and configuration

We may use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config. These services may process app interactions, device and app information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, identifiers, crash logs, stack traces, and diagnostic data. We use this information to operate, secure, configure, and improve the app.

Advertising

The free version may display Google AdMob ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Apple tracking permission, Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, ad interactions, and approximate location to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, or personalize advertising. Where required, we request consent. Users with an active Pro entitlement may receive an ad-free experience.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat helps us manage products, receipts, subscription status, free-trial eligibility, and entitlements. We do not receive your full payment-card details. RevenueCat and Apple may process transaction identifiers, purchase receipts, product and entitlement information, app user identifiers, and related device or technical information.

Location and maps

The app can show map coordinates supplied with its mushroom reference data and can open Apple Maps when you choose navigation. MushroomSnap does not currently use those reference coordinates as your precise device location. Apple Maps is governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Legal pages and network data

When an in-app web page is loaded, the hosting provider may receive standard request information such as IP address, browser or device details, timestamps, and the requested page.

3. Why we use information

  • to provide mushroom identification, reference information, recipes, maps, saved items, and app preferences;
  • to deliver purchases, restore subscriptions, and manage Pro access;
  • to show and measure ads where enabled;
  • to understand usage, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and improve reliability;
  • to comply with law and protect users, our services, and our rights.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only as needed with service providers described above (including Google/Firebase/AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, identification providers, and hosting providers), when you direct us to do so, during a business transfer, or when required to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect safety and rights. Advertising activities may be treated as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some privacy laws.

5. Retention

Local history and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. We keep information under our control only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this policy, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. Service providers apply their own retention periods.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can deny or revoke camera, photo-library, notification, or tracking permission in device Settings.
  • You can choose available consent options for advertising and disable ad personalization through device or regional controls.
  • You can delete locally saved scans and collections in the app or uninstall the app.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability for personal information under our control.

To make a privacy request, email info@vasundhara.io. We may need to verify the request. Because the app does not require an account and much of its content is stored only on your device, we may not be able to identify or retrieve that local content.

7. International processing and security

Our providers may process information in India, the United States, and other countries where privacy laws may differ. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no transmission or storage system is completely secure.

8. Children

MushroomSnap is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied personal information should contact us.

9. Third-party policies
